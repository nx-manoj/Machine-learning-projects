What to do with it

Four decisions follow from the panel, the season and the forecast ceiling

    01
    Never quote a national average without the panel
    The network grew twelve-fold; an unqualified average measures coverage, not air.
    Analysis
    02
    Compare within a season
    A 2.6× annual cycle means a November-to-November comparison is the only fair one.
    Policy
    03
    Time outdoor activity to the afternoon trough
    The 17:00 minimum is 41% below the 22:00 peak — larger than most interventions achieve in a year.
    Public health
    04
    Spend the next effort on data, not models
    Meteorology and fire counts are worth more than any model change tested here.
    Data team

Limits

Seven years of AQI data cannot say which pollutant drove a bad day

  • AQI is an index, not a measurement. It reports the worst sub-index, so it hides which pollutant drove a given day; this dataset carries no pollutant breakdown.
  • One number per city per day. A median across monitors erases the intra-urban variation that determines personal exposure.
  • Station siting changes. A relocated or recalibrated monitor produces exactly the signature Varanasi's 65% fall shows.
Next data to add

Pollutants and weather

PM2.5, PM10 and NO2 say what drove the index; wind, mixing height and fire counts are what would actually beat persistence.
